# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good faith, keep
critique focused on work, and protect private data and credentials. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may remove
content or participation that violates these expectations. This project follows the spirit of the
Contributor Covenant 2.1.
